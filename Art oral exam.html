Conas cleachtadh · How to practise

  1. Abair os ard é. Say every answer out loud before you reveal it. Reading silently doesn’t prepare you for speaking.
  2. Taifead thú féin. Record yourself on your phone and listen back, especially for séimhiú and urú.
  3. Marcáil go hionraic. ✓ Bhí mé ceart or ✗ Ní raibh. Use Le hathbhreithniú to redo only what you got wrong.
  4. Bíodh tuairim agat. Have one opinion sentence ready for each question. Examiners often ask a follow-up.
  5. Scrúdú bréige. In the last few days, do one full practice run a day.

Achoimre · What’s in here

Ceist 1: Cérbh é Dubhghlas de hÍde?
10%Learn the facts, answer the short questions, then work up to the model answer. Don’t learn the model word for word – learn the facts and the verbs, so it sounds like you talking.
Ceist 2: Labhair faoi na téamaí sa léacht.
10%The four themes match the essay. The follow-up questions at the end (opinion, influence, link to Caisleáin Óir) are the ones an examiner is most likely to add.
Ceist 3: Léigh sliocht as Caisleáin Óir os ard.
10%The five samples are on your scrúdú ceisteanna sheet (they aren’t copied here). Use this tab for the sounds and the words you need to know in each sample.
Mínigh na focail agus na frásaí seo ar dhá dhóigh eile i nGaeilge.
Ceist 4 · 10%Read the passage on your sheet first. For each phrase, say two other ways out loud, then reveal. Three ways to build an alternative: (1) swap in another word – anallód → fadó; (2) change the structure – is gráin linn í → tá fuath againn uirthi; (3) say it in plain words – idir dhá thine Bhealtaine → i bponc. My versions are one option each: any correct, natural alternative counts. The handy swaps are at the end.
